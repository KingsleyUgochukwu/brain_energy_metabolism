# A Python-Based Systems Biology Model of Neuronal Energy Metabolism and ATP Demand

## Project Overview

This project develops a computational model of neuronal energy metabolism using Python.

The model investigates how changes in neuronal activity and energy demand influence ATP production and metabolic flux.

## Research Question

How does neuronal energy demand affect ATP production and metabolic flux under different levels of neural activity?

## Objectives

1. Develop a computational model of neuronal energy metabolism.
2. Model ATP production and ATP demand.
3. Investigate the effect of neuronal activity on energy balance.
4. Simulate metabolic flux through major energy-producing pathways.
5. Investigate the effects of metabolic perturbations.
6. Perform sensitivity analysis to identify important metabolic parameters.
